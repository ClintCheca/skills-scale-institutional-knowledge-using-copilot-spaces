# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

These documents describe OctoAcme's end-to-end project management lifecycle, from initiation through planning, execution, release, and retrospective improvement. The process emphasizes iterative delivery, clear ownership, and measurable outcomes so teams can move quickly while maintaining quality.

Roles and responsibilities are defined across project managers, product managers, developers, QA/testing, and stakeholders. These roles work together through regular planning, status updates, risk reviews, and decision checkpoints to keep delivery aligned with business goals.

Communication and risk management are treated as continuous activities, with weekly cadences, escalation paths, and a shared risk register. Quality is maintained through acceptance criteria, testing expectations, review practices, CI checks, security scanning, and release readiness gates.
